Our Heritage

The Journey Began

Founded in 1998, Gourmet Haven began as a small family bistro with a passion for authentic flavors. What started as a modest 20-seat restaurant has grown into one of the city's most beloved dining destinations.

Over the decades, we have evolved while staying true to our roots—fresh ingredients, traditional techniques, and a warm, welcoming atmosphere that makes every guest feel like family.
